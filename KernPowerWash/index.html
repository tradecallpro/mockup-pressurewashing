Selling a Home in Bakersfield? The Outside Is the First Showing.

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Kern Power Wash.

Buyers in Bakersfield and across Kern County decide a lot before they ever step inside. They see the listing photos, then they pull up to the curb. If the stucco is dusty, the driveway is stained, and the windows are cloudy, that first impression costs you.

1. Start with a house wash

Wind and dust bake onto stucco, siding, and trim all year. A full exterior wash lifts it off and makes the whole house look newer. Many realtors tell sellers to do this one first.

2. Don't forget the concrete and the patio

Driveways, walkways, patios, and fences show wear fast. Washing them clean makes the yard look cared for and gives your listing photos a cleaner backdrop.

3. Finish with clean windows

Clear glass lets in more light and makes every room look bigger. If the home has solar panels, getting them cleaned is a smart add-on, since buyers like to see panels that look well kept.

4. Book early, before photos

Wash first, photograph second. Most home washes are done in a single visit, so a call or text a few days before the photographer arrives is usually enough. Serving Bakersfield, Delano, Shafter, Wasco, and the rest of Kern County.
